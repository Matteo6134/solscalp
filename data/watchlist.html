What the gate settles

Passed means the creator probably cannot burn, freeze, tax or block your tokens. It says nothing about whether the price holds — and a soft rug, a dev quietly selling into buyers, breaks no rule and passes every check here.

Why so many collapse

Solidus Labs found 98.6% of 7M+ pump.fun tokens fell below $1k liquidity or were outright rugs. A register this brutal is the base rate showing up, not a fault in the filter.

Sample size

No token has yet aged past the 24-hour mark needed for a verdict, so no rate is quoted. The change column is what happened so far, not a conclusion.
